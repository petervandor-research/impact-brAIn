How this really works

Who is behind impact brAIn?

Me, Peter Vandor. I direct the Center for Social Entrepreneurship and Social Innovation at WU Vienna and have worked on impact measurement for social enterprises for more than a decade. impact brAIn is an MVP and part of my teaching at WU Vienna: an experiment to test whether an AI-first, human-checked process can make good impact work affordable for young social enterprises.

What does the AI do, and what do I do?

Claude reads your material and drafts: IOOI models, indicator sets, survey questions, report text, and a screen of your impact claims. I decide what goes out. I correct drafts, reject weak ones, and add judgement on research quality, for example whether a claimed effect is backed by a baseline or a comparison.

Does it start with a conversation?

Yes, always. Every engagement starts with a call to explore the scope: what you need, who will use the results, and which modules fit.

What happens to my data?

Requests are visible only to me. Your input is processed with Claude, a model by Anthropic. Please do not send personal data about your beneficiaries; aggregated or anonymised information is enough for every module.

What does it cost?

impact brAIn is in a pilot phase. Scope and any fee are agreed with you in writing before work starts. Sending a request commits you to nothing.
